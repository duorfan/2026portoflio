Mornova: An Adaptive Morning Companion

AI-powered wake-up companion that combines a web assistant with a light-based physical device.

Role: Product Designer & Product Manager
‍
Duration: 2 semesters
‍
Focus: Product strategy, UX, conversational design, hardware–software integration
‍

Why I Built Mornova

Mornova started with a simple question I kept asking myself during grad school:

Why do we still wake up to alarms that ignore everything happening in our lives?

Some days I’d have a 9 AM meeting and wake up panicked.
Other days I’d stay up late finishing work and still get jolted awake by a rigid alarm.
Nothing adjusted.
Nothing understood my schedule.
And nothing reduced that “morning anxiety” feeling.
Mornova became my attempt to redesign the morning experience from the ground up, combining AI reasoning, context awareness, and physical light cues into one gentle system.

A Fully Functional Prototype

You can explore it directly inside the mobile mockup on my page: the assistant responds, pulls your calendar and weather data, and proposes wake-up routines based on context.This wasn’t just UI exploration; it was grounding the concept in actual behavior.
